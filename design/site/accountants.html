Questions from firms.

Can clients keep using their own package?

Boeklite works best when the client books in Boeklite. You can import data from other packages to start.

Who pays for the client's subscription?

Either. The firm can pay for clients on a firm plan, or clients pay their own plan and invite you for free.

Can I set up roles for my team?

Yes. Give team members access per client and choose who can approve and who can file.

Do you offer onboarding for firms?

Yes. We help you set up your first clients and train your team.

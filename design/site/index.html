Questions, answered.

Is Boeklite suitable for a BV as well as a ZZP?

Yes. Boeklite supports eenmanszaak, VOF and BV administrations, with the grootboek and BTW codes set up for each.

Can my accountant work in my administration?

Yes. Invite your accountant by email. They see the same books, can review entries and prepare the return with you.

How does the BTW-aangifte work?

Boeklite fills each rubriek from your booked entries. You check the amounts, click through to the source entries if you want, and submit.

Can I switch from another bookkeeping package?

Yes. Import your opening balance and last year's bank lines, and you are ready to go. We help you with the move.

Where is my data stored?

In data centres within the European Union. See the Security page for details on encryption, backups and access.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan, or your account stays read-only so you can always export your data.
